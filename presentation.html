Problem Context & Industry Fit
The Rigid EMI Poverty Trap vs. Adaptive Solvency
Why 2 billion informal earners face artificial default under traditional credit scoring
❌ The Status Quo: Rigid EMI Trap
  • ✕
    Cyclical Revenue Shocks: In agriculture and artisan crafts, earnings fluctuate naturally. Rigid monthly EMIs trigger default during lean sowing months even when annual net income is strong.
  • ✕
    Information Asymmetry: Conventional bureaus cannot mathematically distinguish a temporary 60-day medical illness shock from irreversible bankruptcy.
  • ✕
    Destructive Default Spiral: Borrowers get blacklisted and turn to predatory loan sharks; lenders suffer high non-performing loans (NPLs) and legal recovery write-downs.
✓ Our Solution: Adaptive Credit
  • ✓
    Empirical Trajectory Diagnostics: Evaluates 24-month granular cashflows, isolating YoY autocorrelation, rolling buffers, and peak-to-trough spreads.
  • ✓
    Trajectory Disambiguation: Statistically separates temporary shocks (≤3 months with proven rebound) from structural decline (>20% sustained decay).
  • ✓
    Tri-Axial Optimization Matrix: Simulates 5 restructuring plans to balance Borrower Affordability (45%), Lender Capital Recovery (35%), and Stability (20%).
System Architecture
Dual-Layer "Defense in Depth": Rules + ML + LLM
Why credit committees require auditable statistical layers rather than unverified black boxes

Translates mathematical evidence chains into credit committee memos. Strictly protected by verify_grounding() hallucination guard and 0ms deterministic fallback.

    The Golden Rule: "The LLM never computes numbers; it only translates grounded math into human language."
