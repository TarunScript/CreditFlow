The Core Dilemma: Rigid EMIs vs. Adaptive Credit

Why conventional microfinance triggers preventable defaults and how dynamic restructuring fixes it:

The Status Quo: The Rigid EMI Poverty Trap

  • Cyclical Shocks Trigger Defaults: In agriculture and artisan trades, income drops naturally during monsoons or sowing periods. Rigid EMIs force default even when annual earnings are strong.
  • Lender Information Asymmetry: Traditional micro-lenders cannot mathematically distinguish a 2-month medical shock from permanent business decline.
  • Loss on Both Sides: Borrowers face blacklisting and debt distress; lenders suffer preventable non-performing loans (NPLs) and expensive legal recoveries.

Our Solution: Adaptive Cash-Flow Restructuring

  • Empirical Trajectory Separation: Distinguishes temporary downturns (≤3 months with post-shock recovery) from structural decline (>20% sustained decay).
  • Tri-Axial Optimization Matrix: Computes 5 restructuring options, optimizing Borrower Affordability against Lender Recovery Rate.
  • Sustainable Solvency (UN SDG 8): Replaces flat EMIs with seasonal steps, income links, or temporary relief grace periods that keep default rates near zero.
